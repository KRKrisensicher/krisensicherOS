# BCMS Canvas for Critical Processes

## Purpose

This template records which process is critical, what it depends on, what impact an outage has, and which decision is needed next.

It is a working artifact for decisions and evidence. It does not replace legal advice, data protection advice, or a management decision.

## Trigger

Use when BCMS readiness or resilience work for critical processes is started.

## Users

- responsible owner of the routine
- information security officer/CISO or security responsible
- involved process, service, or control owners
- reviewer or management review facilitator

## Input

- scope and non-scope
- relevant risks, findings, requirements, or goals
- existing routines, controls, or evidence
- owner and involved roles
- open decisions or escalation points

## Flow

1. Clarify the goal and scope of the template.
2. Enter relevant information as references; do not copy confidential full texts.
3. Add owner, trigger, output, and evidence reference.
4. Mark gaps, assumptions, and decision needs.
5. Define priority or review cadence.
6. Document the human review point and next step.

## Output

- completed working artifact
- open decisions
- evidence list
- prioritized follow-up actions
- review or escalation note

## Evidence

The completed template, review notes, decision log, action list, or references to evidence packs are created as evidence. Confidential evidence is referenced only in private user environments.

## Limits

- does not provide legal advice
- does not provide data protection advice
- does not provide certification or compliance assurance
- no management decision by agents
- no real personal, customer, or organizational data in public examples
- no reproduction of licensed standard texts or confidential contract content

## Review

Review when the scope changes, new risks, new requirements, incidents, management decisions, or at least according to the cadence defined for the routine.
