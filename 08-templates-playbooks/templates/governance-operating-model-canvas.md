# Governance Operating Model Canvas

## Purpose

This canvas translates requirements into roles, routines, decisions, evidence, and reviews. It is not a legal opinion and does not provide compliance confirmation.

## Trigger

Use when new requirements, findings, risks, or management objectives need to be translated into operable governance.

## Users

- information security officer/CISO or security leads
- governance/compliance coordination
- process and service owners
- management review preparation

## Input

- scope and out of scope
- source or register entries as references
- existing roles and committees
- known risks, findings, or measures
- available evidence sources

## Process

1. Define the scope and objective of the governance routine.
2. Cluster requirements by topic.
3. Name owners, reviewers, and decision-makers.
4. Describe routines with trigger, frequency, input, output, and evidence.
5. Mark escalations and management decisions.
6. Check workload: remove anything that has no decision or evidence value.
7. Define review cadence.

## Output

- roles/routines draft
- evidence flow sketch
- management decision questions
- measures or improvement backlog

## Evidence

Evidence produced includes the completed canvas, decision log, measures list, review minutes, or evidence pack reference.

## Boundaries

- does not provide legal advice
- does not provide data protection advice
- no certification or compliance assurance
- no management decision by agents
- no real customer data or confidential content in public examples

## Review

Review at least quarterly or when there are new requirements, significant risks, reorganization, major incidents, or management decisions.
