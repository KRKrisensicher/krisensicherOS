# Prepare Evidence Pack

## Situation / Trigger

Use when a review, measure closure, audit discussion, or management review needs reliable evidence.

## Objective

The playbook compiles an evidence pack so that reviewers can see: which decision is pending, which evidence is available, what is missing, and who will follow up.

## Roles

- Evidence Owner: provides evidence and confirms currency.
- Evidence Pack Reviewer: reviews purpose, quality, gaps, and traceability.
- Control Owner: explains which routine or control the evidence supports.
- Information security officer/CISO: decides whether the evidence is sufficient for the next review or must be escalated.
- Management: decides on resource needs, risk acceptance, or open priorities.

## Input Data

- Review objective and decision question
- List of expected evidence
- Storage locations, owners, and currency date
- Open findings, measures, or assumptions
- Boundaries: confidential, personal, or licensed content

## Process

1. Record the review objective and decision question.
2. Enter evidence with owner, source, date, and purpose in the evidence pack index.
3. Review quality: current, traceable, and sufficient for the next decision.
4. Clearly mark missing evidence, assumptions, and limitations.
5. Transfer management or review questions to the decision log and measures backlog.

## Escalation

Escalate if:

- legal or data protection assessment is needed,
- risk acceptance or budget decision is needed,
- critical evidence is missing,
- roles or owners remain unclear,
- external communication is being prepared,
- live incident or crisis decisions are affected.

## Outputs

- Review or workshop note
- Completed templates
- Measures backlog
- Evidence needs list
- Decision log
- Lessons learned
- Handoff to suitable agents, skills, or human roles

## Follow-up

- Define measures with owner and deadline.
- Update decision log.
- Add to the evidence pack or register entries.
- Set the next review cadence.
- Add lessons learned to the improvement list.

## Boundaries

- does not provide legal advice
- does not provide data protection advice
- does not provide certification or compliance assurance
- no management decision by agents
- no live crisis command
- no real customer, personal, or confidential organizational data in public examples
