# Monthly Security Governance Review

## Situation / Trigger

Use when risks, measures, evidence packs, and open decisions are consolidated monthly.

## Goal

The review shows what has changed since the last session, which measures are blocked, which evidence is missing, and which decisions must be escalated.

## Roles

- Information security officer/CISO: moderates prioritization and escalation.
- Measure owner: reports status, blockers, evidence, and next step.
- Risk owner: reviews changed risks and acceptance questions.
- Evidence reviewer: checks whether evidence is current and findable.
- Executive management representative: decides on priority, budget, or acceptance questions.

## Input Data

- Measure backlog with owner, deadline, and status
- Risk register or open risk notes
- Evidence Pack Index
- New findings, incidents, or changes
- Decisions from the last review

## Process

1. Collect changes since the last review: risks, incidents, findings, scope.
2. Sort measures by overdue, blocked, ready for decision, and completed.
3. Check evidence gaps and assign owners for follow-up work.
4. Formulate management decisions: option, evidence, risk of inaction.
5. Update minutes, decision log, and next review agenda.

## Escalation

Escalate if:

- legal or data protection assessment is needed,
- risk acceptance or a budget decision is needed,
- critical evidence is missing,
- roles or owners remain unclear,
- external communication is being prepared,
- live incident or crisis decisions are affected.

## Outputs

- Review or workshop note
- Completed templates
- Measure backlog
- Evidence Needs List
- Decision Log
- Lessons Learned
- Handoff to suitable agents, skills, or human roles

## Follow-up

- Define measures with owner and deadline.
- Update the decision log.
- Add evidence pack or register entries.
- Set the next review cadence.
- Add lessons learned to the improvement list.

## Boundaries

- does not provide legal advice
- does not provide data protection advice
- does not provide certification or compliance assurance
- no management decision by agents
- no live crisis management
- no real customer, personal, or confidential organizational data in public examples
