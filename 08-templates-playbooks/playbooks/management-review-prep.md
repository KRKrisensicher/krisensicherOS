# Prepare Management Review

## Situation / Trigger

Use when management decisions on risks, measures, resources, or priorities need to be prepared.

## Goal

The playbook condenses evidence, options, and open decisions so that management can decide without having to work through raw material.

## Roles

- Management review facilitator: creates agenda, decision questions, and protocol structure.
- information security officer/CISO: prioritizes risks, measures, and escalations.
- Evidence reviewer: checks whether evidence is sufficient for the decision question.
- Measure owners: provide status, blockers, and resource needs.
- Management: decides acceptance, priorities, budget, escalation, and next reviews.

## Input Data

- open risks, findings, and measures
- evidence pack or review notes
- decision questions and options
- resource needs, blockers, and deadlines
- previous management decisions and open follow-ups

## Process

1. Derive decision questions from risks, findings, and measures.
2. Summarize evidence and open assumptions for each decision.
3. Prepare options with impact, effort, risk of inaction, and owner.
4. Shorten the agenda to the decisions that need management approval.
5. Track decisions, conditions, deadlines, and review date in the decision log.

## Escalation

Escalate if:

- legal or data protection assessment is needed,
- risk acceptance or budget decision is needed,
- critical evidence is missing,
- roles or owners remain unclear,
- external communication is being prepared,
- live incident or crisis decisions are affected.

## Outputs

- review or workshop note
- completed templates
- measures backlog
- evidence needs list
- decision log
- lessons learned
- handoff to appropriate agents, skills, or human roles

## Follow-up

- Define measures with owner and deadline.
- Update decision log.
- Add evidence pack or register entries.
- Set the next review cadence.
- Transfer lessons learned to the improvement list.

## Boundaries

- does not provide legal advice
- does not provide data protection advice
- does not provide certification or compliance assurance
- no management decision by agents
- no live crisis management
- no real customer, personal, or confidential organizational data in public examples
