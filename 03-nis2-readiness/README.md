# 03 NIS2 Readiness

This module supports NIS2 preliminary scoping, gap analysis, measure prioritization, notification readiness, evidence logic, and management communication.

**Operating logic:** Translates regulatory pressure into steerable work packages, reviewable evidence, and clear management decisions. It does not replace legal advice or binding applicability assessment.

## Start here

1. If scope is unclear, start with the `NIS2 preliminary applicability questionnaire` (`templates/nis2-vorab-betroffenheitspruefung-fragebogen.md`).
2. Use the `NIS2 readiness start workshop` (`playbooks/nis2-readiness-startworkshop.md`) when management mandate, roles, and first scope need clarification.
3. Capture gaps in the `NIS2 gap worksheet` (`templates/nis2-gap-worksheet.md`).
4. Connect findings with evidence and decisions through `06 Evidence & Management Review` (`../06-evidence-management-review/README.md`).
5. Practice notification and escalation logic with `nis2-incident-melde-triage.md` (`playbooks/nis2-incident-melde-triage.md`).

## Operating flow

`Capture gap → assess risk/controls in 04 → create evidence requests in 06 → prepare management review → update decision log`

## Key artifacts

- `readiness/nis2-erwaegungsgruende-implementierungslogik.md` (`readiness/nis2-erwaegungsgruende-implementierungslogik.md`) — translate recitals into operating logic.
- `workflows/nis2-readiness-gap.yaml` (`workflows/nis2-readiness-gap.yaml`) — structured gap workflow.
- `workflows/nis2-vorab-betroffenheitspruefung.yaml` (`workflows/nis2-vorab-betroffenheitspruefung.yaml`) — preliminary assessment workflow with legal handoff.
- `templates/nis2-management-review-und-schulung.md` (`templates/nis2-management-review-und-schulung.md`) — prepare executive training and review.
- `../02-governance-operating-model/templates/legal-datenschutz-handoff.md` (`../02-governance-operating-model/templates/legal-datenschutz-handoff.md`) — handoff for legal/data protection questions.

## Human gates

Applicability, legal interpretation, notification deadlines, external communication, risk acceptance, and executive management decisions must be reviewed and approved by accountable humans.

## Typical outputs

- preliminary scope with legal handoff,
- prioritized gaps,
- evidence requests,
- management decisions in the decision log,
- escalation and notification exercise without a live crisis-management claim.
