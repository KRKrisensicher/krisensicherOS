# Legal/Data Protection Handoff

## Purpose

This template creates a structured handoff artifact when krisensicherOS work touches legal, data protection, or contractual questions.

It does not replace legal advice or a data protection assessment. It only helps hand over the right question, with context, to the responsible human roles.

## Trigger

Use this handoff when:

- a legal interpretation may be needed,
- personal data is to be processed,
- contractual, customer, or supplier requirements are affected,
- licensed standard texts or confidential requirements play a role,
- AI use is planned for sensitive content,
- external communication or publication is being prepared.

## Roles

- Requesting role: CISO/information security officer, GRC, audit, business unit.
- Review role: Legal, data protection, contract management, or management.
- Decision role: responsible human, not agent or AI.

## Separation of Questions

### Security/Governance Question

What needs to be clarified organizationally, technically, or procedurally?

### Legal Question

Which legal, contractual, or regulatory interpretation must be performed by responsible humans?

### Data Protection Question

Which personal processing, legal basis, transparency, purpose limitation, retention, or recipient question must be reviewed?

### Management Question

Which resource, priority, risk, or acceptance decision remains with management?

## Output

- clean handoff to Legal/Data Protection/Management,
- open questions instead of asserted interpretation,
- documented stop or approval point,
- handoff back to the governance, audit, or evidence workflow.

## Quality Gates

- The question is concrete and formulated so it can be decided.
- Context is sufficient, but not unnecessarily confidential.
- Personal or confidential content is minimized.
- No legal or data protection assessment is anticipated.
- The result is documented as human feedback.

## Limits

This template does not create legal advice, data protection advice, compliance assurance, or a management decision.
