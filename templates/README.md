# Templates

Operational templates with operating logic. No template without user role, trigger, decision point, evidence, and review logic.


## Templates v1.0

- `compliance-source-register.md` — Structure sources and requirements as a register entry.
- `governance-operating-model-canvas.md` — Design roles, routines, decisions, and evidence.
- `nis2-vorab-betroffenheitspruefung-fragebogen.md` — Structure NIS2 applicability indicators in advance; always have the result reviewed by legal counsel/Legal.
- `nis2-gap-worksheet.md` — Capture NIS2/requirement gaps.
- `audit-questionnaire.md` — Structure audit guiding questions.
- `audit-test-program.md` — Create an audit test program.
- `evidence-request-list.md` — Request evidence specifically.
- `control-test-plan.md` — Plan control tests.
- `risikoanalyse-fragebogen.md` — Capture the current measures situation with AI assistance and confirm it professionally.
- `risikoanalyse-session-state.md` — Record and continue the status of a guided risk analysis wizard.
- `risikoanalyse-register.md` — Capture risks by vulnerability + asset + threat with gross and net/residual risk assessment.
- `soa-risk-control-map.md` — Connect risks, existing/planned measures, control/SoA decisions, and evidence.
- `risikoreport.md` — Condense risk, measure, control/SoA, and decision status for reviews.
- `risikoreport-html.html` — HTML presentation for management review or reporting.
- `audit-finding-report.md` — Document findings and deviations.
- `corrective-action-plan.md` — Plan measures from findings.
- `remediation-effectiveness-review.md` — Review measure effectiveness.
- `evidence-pack-index.md` — Structure evidence packs.
- `management-review-agenda.md` — Prepare management review.
- `nis2-incident-melde-triage.md` — Prepare NIS2/BSIG/special-regime notification assessment as incident triage.
- `nis2-management-review-und-schulung.md` — Structure executive management training, management review, decision log, and evidence pack.
- `decision-log.md` — Record decisions and open items.
- `ki-nutzungsfreigabe-matrix.md` — Clarify AI usage, data classes, redaction, and human gates before tool use.
- `ki-system-inventar-und-risikovorpruefung.md` — Inventory AI systems, data classes, human oversight, and EU AI Act handoff questions.
- `legal-datenschutz-handoff.md` — Hand over legal, data protection, contract, and confidentiality questions in a structured way to the responsible humans.

All templates remain aids. Legal review, data protection assessment, risk acceptance, and management decisions remain human gates.
