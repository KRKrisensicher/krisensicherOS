# NIS2 Gap Worksheet

## Purpose

This worksheet structures NIS2 readiness gaps as verifiable working logic: reference, scope, existing routine, evidence, gap, action, priority, and human decision. It does not confirm regulatory fulfillment.

## Trigger

Use for NIS2 readiness review, workshops, findings, management review preparation, or building an action backlog.

## Users

- information security officer/CISO or security leads
- Regulatory Source Mapper
- Risk and Obligation Prioritizer
- Control Evidence Architect
- Management Review-Facilitator

## Input

- scope and non-scope
- public source references or compliance register entries
- existing routines and controls
- existing evidence
- known findings or risks

## Process

1. Record the reference as source/location/metadata; do not copy full standard or legal texts.
2. Assign working field.
3. Review existing routine and evidence.
4. Formulate the gap concretely.
5. Derive action with proposed owner and evidence output.
6. Justify priority.
7. Mark human review or management decision.

## Output

- prioritized gap list
- action backlog
- evidence needs list
- management decision questions

## Evidence

Evidence includes the completed worksheet, action log, source/register reference, and review note.

## Boundaries

- does not provide legal advice
- does not provide data protection advice
- does not provide compliance assurance
- no decision on legal applicability
- no confidential or licensed content in public artifacts

## Review

Review when there are new sources, national implementation changes, scope changes, major incidents, audit findings, or at least quarterly during build-up phases.
