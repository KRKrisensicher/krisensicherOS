# ISMS Scope Canvas

## Purpose

This canvas helps to define an initial ISMS scope in a traceable way and connect it with risks, roles, controls, evidence, and review logic. It does not provide certification assurance or compliance assurance.

## Trigger

Use when building a minimum viable ISMS, during scope expansions, management reviews, or when security work becomes too broad and document-heavy.

## Users

- information security officer/CISO or ISMS responsible person
- service and process owners
- Risk and Obligation Prioritizer
- Control Evidence Architect
- Management Review-Facilitator

## Input

- organization or service scope candidates
- critical processes, information, and dependencies
- known risks and findings
- existing controls and evidence sources
- management objectives or resource framework

## Process

1. Describe the purpose of the initial ISMS scope.
2. Define scope and non-scope.
3. Enter critical assets, processes, and dependencies.
4. Mark central risk topics.
5. Review existing controls as routines.
6. Add evidence and review needs.
7. Prepare the scope decision for management review.

## Output

- scope draft
- non-scope and expansion criteria
- risk/control mapping
- decision questions for management review

## Evidence

Evidence produced includes the scope canvas, decision log, risk/control map, and review note.

## Boundaries

- does not provide legal advice
- does not provide data protection advice
- does not provide certification assurance
- no adoption of licensed standard texts
- no real organizational data in public examples

## Review

Review for new services, organizational change, material risks, new requirements, incidents, or at least annually.
