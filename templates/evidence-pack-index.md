# Evidence Pack Index

## Purpose

The Evidence Pack Index bundles evidence so that reviews, management decisions, and improvement work become possible. It is not proof of complete fulfillment of regulatory or normative requirements.

## Trigger

Use before management reviews, internal reviews, measure closure, audit preparation, incident follow-up, or gap closure.

## Users

- Control owner
- Evidence pack reviewer
- information security officer/CISO or security responsible person
- Management review facilitator
- internal audit or quality function as reviewer

## Input

- Scope
- Control or routine
- Evidence types and storage locations
- Currency requirement
- Review questions
- open findings or measures

## Process

1. Assign the evidence pack to a scope, control, or review objective.
2. Record evidence with source, owner, date, status, and purpose.
3. Check quality: current, traceable, and sufficient for the next decision.
4. Mark gaps, assumptions, and constraints.
5. Document review result and follow-up actions.
6. Escalate management decisions when evidence shows gaps or resource needs.

## Output

- Evidence Pack Index
- Gap and follow-up list
- Review note
- Management decision questions

## Evidence

The index is itself review evidence. It points to evidence but does not copy confidential content into public artifacts.

## Boundaries

- do not copy confidential evidence into public examples
- do not process personal data without approval
- do not derive any compliance or security assurance
- do not replace any audit or management decision
- do not reproduce licensed content

## Review

Review depending on risk and routine: typically monthly for measures, quarterly for controls, event-based after incidents or before management reviews.
