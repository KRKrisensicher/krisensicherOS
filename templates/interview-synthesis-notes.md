# Interview Synthesis

## Purpose

This template organizes interview and workshop notes by statements, assumptions, gaps, evidence needs, and next review points. It does not replace a legal, data protection, audit, or management decision.

## Trigger

Use when interviews or workshops are being evaluated.

## Users

- responsible owner
- internal audit, governance, ISMS, or compliance role
- evidence reviewer
- management review facilitator
- subject-matter reviewers or approval owners

## Input

- scope and out-of-scope
- requirements, source, or register references
- existing documents, controls, evidence, or raw notes
- roles, owners, and review objective
- assessment or approval criteria

## Process

1. Define the scope and objective of the template.
2. Capture criteria, requirements, or raw material as references.
3. Map to questions, methods, document sections, evidence, or actions.
4. Mark gaps, assumptions, deviations, or open review questions.
5. Add owner, human gate, and next step.
6. Check the result against public safety and claim safety.

## Output

- completed template
- mapping between requirement, evidence, method, document, or action
- open questions and human gates
- follow-up or action list
- handoff to appropriate agents or human roles

## Evidence

The completed template, review notes, decision log, action plan, or references to evidence packs are created as evidence. Confidential content remains in private user environments.

## Boundaries

- does not provide legal advice
- does not provide data protection advice
- does not provide audit, certification, or compliance assurance
- no management decision by agents
- no real personal, customer, or organizational data in public examples
- no reproduction of licensed standard texts or confidential contract content

## Review

Review by the responsible human role before use in an audit, management review, document approval, external communication, or action closure.
