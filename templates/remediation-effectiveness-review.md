# Remediation Effectiveness Review

## Purpose

This template checks whether a completed action has actually been implemented in a traceable way and is effective enough to continue processing, close, or re-escalate a finding, gap, or risk.

## Trigger

- Corrective Action has been reported as completed,
- audit finding is to be closed,
- management review requires action status,
- recurring deviation occurs again.

## User roles

- action owner,
- finding owner,
- information security officer/CISO or GRC responsible,
- internal audit / audit responsible,
- management for risk or resource decisions.

| --- | --- |

| remaining gap |  |

| recommendation | close / keep open / reopen / management decision |
| human gate |  |

## Review questions

- Was the committed action actually implemented?
- Does the evidence show only activity or also a changed routine?
- Is the cause addressed or only the symptom?
- Are there new side effects, workload, or control gaps?
- Who may approve closure, risk acceptance, or further escalation?

## Output

- effectiveness review,
- closure/reopen recommendation,
- open evidence requests,
- handoff to management review or action backlog.

## Boundaries

No guarantee that a risk has been eliminated. Does not provide compliance, certification, or audit assurance. Closure and risk acceptance remain human gates.
