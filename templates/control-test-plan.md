# Control Test Plan

## Purpose

This template makes controls testable: test objective, method, sample, evidence, assessment notes, and escalation.

## Triggers

- Control is to be tested internally,
- audit question requires a concrete test action,
- measure or finding requires evidence of effectiveness,
- management review needs a robust decision basis.

## User roles

- Control owner,
- internal audit / audit leads,
- information security officer/CISO or GRC leads,
- evidence reviewer.

## Test steps

1. Confirm scope and owner.
2. Describe criterion and expected state.
3. Request evidence and check quality.
4. Perform test action.
5. Document result, deviation, assumption, or gap.
6. Prepare handoff to finding, corrective action, or management review.

## Output

- Control test note,
- evidence list,
- open questions,
- finding or measure handoff.

## Quality gates

- Public safety,
- claim safety,
- evidence reference,
- operating logic,
- human review.

## Boundaries

No external audit assurance, no legal or data protection advice, no certification or compliance guarantee.
