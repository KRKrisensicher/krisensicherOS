# Plan a Ransomware Tabletop

## Situation / Trigger

Use when an organization wants to prepare a fictional ransomware exercise with learning objectives, roles, injects, and an after-action review.

## Objective

The playbook creates a safe exercise structure: scenario, roles, injects, observation points, decisions, and follow-up. It does not replace live crisis management.

## Roles

- Exercise lead: steers the process, schedule, injects, and safety boundaries.
- Crisis team lead: practices situation awareness, decisions, and escalations.
- IT/security specialist roles: assess the technical situation and recovery options.
- Communications/legal/data protection: review simulated notification and communication points.
- Observers: document decisions, blockers, evidence, and improvements.

## Input Data

- Exercise objective and non-objective
- fictional scenario without real customer data or secrets
- role list and communication channels
- existing incident, crisis, and recovery plans
- observation sheet and after-action review structure

## Process

1. Define learning objectives and abort criteria.
2. Describe the scenario and injects as fictional, public-safe, and without real systems.
3. Clarify roles, decision rights, and communication channels for the exercise.
4. Conduct the exercise and observe decisions, assumptions, blockers, and evidence needs.
5. Conclude the after-action review with actions, owners, deadlines, and the next exercise point.

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- legal or data protection assessment is needed,
- risk acceptance or a budget decision is needed,
- critical evidence is missing,
- roles or owners remain unclear,
- external communication is being prepared,
- live incident or crisis decisions are affected.

## Outputs

- Review or workshop note
- completed templates
- action backlog
- Evidence Needs List
- Decision Log
- Lessons Learned
- Handoff to suitable agents, skills, or human roles

## Follow-up

- Define actions with owner and deadline.
- Update the Decision Log.
- Add Evidence Pack or register entries.
- Set the next review cadence.
- Transfer Lessons Learned into the improvement list.

## Boundaries

- does not provide legal advice
- does not provide data protection advice
- does not provide certification or compliance assurance
- no management decision by agents
- no live crisis management
- no real customer, personal, or confidential organizational data in public examples
