# Role Matrix

## Purpose

The role matrix makes visible who operates a governance routine, who contributes, who reviews, and who decides. It does not replace formal role approval by management.

## Trigger

Use for unclear responsibilities, new requirements, ISMS/NIS2/BCMS setup, evidence pack work, or management review preparation.

## Users

- information security officer/CISO or security leads
- process and service owners
- management review facilitator
- internal audit or quality function as reviewer

## Input

- scope
- relevant requirements or register entries
- existing roles/bodies
- affected processes or services
- open decisions

## Process

1. Name the routine or artifact.
2. Define a human owner or mark as an open decision.
3. Add contributors and reviewers.
4. Mark the decision role and escalation path.
5. Define the evidence output of the role.
6. Make conflicts and overload visible.

## Output

- draft roles and responsibilities
- open owner decisions
- escalation and review points
- input for management decision brief

## Evidence

Evidence includes the role matrix, approvals/change history, review note, and, if applicable, decision log.

## Boundaries

- do not automate any management decision
- do not prescribe any legal assessment of responsibility
- do not enter personal data in public examples
- do not mark roles as final without real capacity

## Review

Review upon role changes, scope changes, new requirements, incidents, audit findings, or at least semi-annually.
