# Disclaimer

krisensicherOS is an open working and structuring system for security governance, NIS2 readiness, ISMS basics, evidence work, and crisis readiness.

The content does not provide legal advice. It does not replace organization-specific legal review, certification advice, authority interpretation, or expert approval by responsible persons.

Agents, skills, templates, and playbooks can prepare, structure, review, and accelerate work. Decisions, approvals, risk acceptances, and assumption of responsibility remain with the responsible people and bodies of the respective organization.
