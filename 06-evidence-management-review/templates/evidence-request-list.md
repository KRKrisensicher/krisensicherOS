# Evidence Request List

## Purpose

This template structures evidence requests for audits, gap assessments, control tests, evidence packs, and management reviews.

## Trigger

- new or changed requirement,
- internal audit or self-assessment,
- missing evidence in an evidence pack,
- preparation of a control test or management review.

## User Roles

- audit leads,
- information security officer/CISO or GRC leads,
- process or control owners,
- evidence reviewers.

## Input Data

- scope and criterion,
- requirement / control / finding,
- existing evidence,
- owners and deadlines,
- confidentiality or license notes.

## Decision Filter

Every request needs an answer to: **Why is this evidence needed for the next decision?**

If this question cannot be answered, the request is not made or is moved to a later expansion stage.

## Workload Rule

For the first end-to-end pass:

- maximum of three requests per owner,
- reuse existing evidence first,
- request new evidence only if it is decision-relevant,
- make effort visible,
- avoid unnecessary burden on business units.

## Quality Criteria

- Evidence is described concretely, not just “provide document”.
- Purpose and decision relevance of the evidence are visible.
- Owner and deadline are named or marked as a gap.
- Confidentiality and license boundaries have been checked.
- Missing evidence is not replaced by assumptions.

## Output

- Evidence Request List,
- open owner/scope questions,
- handoff to evidence pack or audit program.

## Human Gate

Human review is required for confidential content, personal data, legal interpretation, data protection assessment, risk acceptance, and external communication.

## Boundaries

Does not provide legal advice, does not provide data protection advice, does not provide compliance or certification assurance, no reproduction of licensed standard texts.
