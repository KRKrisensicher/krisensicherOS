# 09 — Monitoring, Management Review, and Decisions

## Purpose

This module makes ISMS status, risks, actions, evidence, and open decisions manageable for leadership.

## Reference Framework

- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 — Sections 9.1 and 9.3.
- BSIG § 38 where applicability is possible.

## Users and Roles

- executive management,
- management review facilitator,
- ISMS owner,
- risk owner,
- evidence owner,
- action owner,
- legal/data protection for open obligations.

## Triggers

- regular management review,
- new top risks,
- overdue actions,
- evidence gaps,
- incident or finding,
- resource or risk acceptance question.

## Process

1. Consolidate status from risks, actions, incidents, suppliers, and evidence packs.
2. Formulate decision questions.
3. Prepare options with impact, effort, risk of inaction, and owner.
4. Document management decision.
5. Set follow-up, deadlines, and review date.

## Decisions

| Decision | Options | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accept risk? | no / time-limited / permanent with review | Risk Brief |
| Prioritize action? | now / later / not | action status |
| Release resources? | yes / no / alternative option | Decision Brief |
| Change scope? | expand / limit / assess | Scope Review |
| Management training needed? | yes / refresher / no | training status |

## Evidence

- management review agenda,
- Decision Brief,
- decision log,
- action status,
- risk report,
- evidence pack index,
- training evidence.

## Review

Management reviews must lead to decisions. Pure status meetings without a decision, condition, or escalation create little governance value.

## BSIG/NIS2 Reference

Where applicability is possible, this module supports executive responsibility, training logic, and monitoring. It does not replace legal review.

## Boundaries

Agents prepare, but do not decide risk acceptance, budgets, external communication, or interpretation of obligations.

## Handoffs

- to executive management,
- to action owner,
- to risk owner,
- to legal/data protection,
- to evidence reviewer.
