# 05 — Competence, Communication, and Evidence

## Purpose

This module ensures that roles are able to act, relevant communication is steered, and evidence remains findable.

## Reference Framework

- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 — Sections 7.2 to 7.5.
- BSIG § 38 for executive management training in case of possible applicability.

## Users and Roles

- ISMS owner,
- HR/training,
- executive management,
- communication role,
- evidence owner,
- data protection/legal for sensitive communication.

## Triggers

- new role in the ISMS,
- new or changed routine,
- training need,
- management review,
- incident or notification exercise,
- evidence pack incomplete.

## Process

1. Describe role-specific competence requirements: What must the role be able to decide or execute?
2. Define training and briefing routine.
3. Define communication channels: internal, management, business unit, supplier, customer, authority.
4. Define evidence flows: What is created, where is it stored, who reviews it?
5. Consider confidentiality and data classes.
6. Review the evidence pack regularly.

## Decisions

| Decision | Decision-Maker | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Is training needed? | ISMS owner / management | Training plan |
| Who may communicate externally? | Management / legal / communication | Communication approval |
| Which evidence is sufficient? | Evidence owner / reviewer | Evidence pack index |
| Which data may be used in AI work? | Data protection / management / IT | AI usage approval matrix |

## Evidence

- Training records,
- briefing notes,
- communication approvals,
- evidence pack index,
- document and evidence register,
- review notes.

## Review

Check whether roles are not only named but enabled. Evidence must be current, findable, and usable for decisions.

## BSIG/NIS2 Reference

In case of possible applicability, management training, notification communication, and evidence capability require particular attention. This module prepares the operating logic for that.

## Boundaries

No communication approval by agents, no data protection or legal assessment, no storage of confidential evidence in the public repo.

## Handoffs

- to management review,
- to data protection/legal,
- to HR/training,
- to incident communication,
- to evidence reviewer.
