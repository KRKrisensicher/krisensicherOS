# 00 — Use and Boundaries

## Purpose

This module clarifies how the ISMS implementation guide is used. The ISMS is understood here as an operating routine: roles make decisions, routines run, evidence emerges from work, reviews lead to improvements.

## Reference Framework

- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 — Clauses 4 to 10 and Annex A as reference anchors.
- BSIG: <https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bsig_2025/>
- BSIG § 28 and Annexes 1/2 for possible applicability assessment.
- BSIG § 30, § 32, and § 38, if the organization could be in scope.
- EnWG § 5c to § 5e only where energy is relevant.

The references do not replace interpretation of standards or legal review.

## Users and Roles

- Executive management: mandate, resources, risk acceptance.
- ISMS owner: keeps scope, backlog, review, and evidence together.
- Information security officer/CISO: steers subject matter and prepares decisions.
- Risk owner: assesses risks and residual risks.
- Control owner: operates safeguards as a working practice.
- Evidence owner: keeps evidence findable and current.
- Legal/data protection: review legal and data protection questions.

## Triggers

Use for:

- building or restarting an ISMS,
- unclear role or decision logic,
- NIS2/BSIG preliminary check,
- management review,
- findings, incidents, or scope changes,
- preparation for internal reviews.

## Process

1. Define the purpose of the ISMS: Which decisions should it enable?
2. Review AI usage, data classes, and human gates.
3. Select modules; do not work on everything at once.
4. Transfer results into the decision log, risk register, measures backlog, and evidence pack.
5. Document subject-matter, legal, and management approvals.

## Decisions

| Decision | Typical Decision-Maker | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ISMS objective and mode of use | Executive management / ISMS owner | Mandate note |
| approved data class for AI work | Management / data protection / IT | AI usage approval matrix |
| Scope starting point | Executive management / ISMS owner | Scope canvas |
| Review cadence | ISMS owner / management | Review plan |

## Evidence

- Mandate or assignment,
- role list,
- AI usage approval matrix,
- decision log,
- list of open human gates,
- module status and next reviews.

## Review

Check questions:

- Is it clear who decides and who only prepares?
- Does evidence emerge from real routines?
- Are legal, data protection, and management decisions separated?
- Are references to standards and laws used only as reference anchors?

## BSIG/NIS2 Reference

Where applicability is possible, the organization must additionally have it reviewed whether BSIG obligations are relevant. This guide prepares roles, risk steering, notification triage, and evidence capability for that purpose, but does not determine applicability.

## Boundaries

Does not provide legal advice, does not provide data protection advice, does not provide certification assurance, does not interpret standards, and does not assume management responsibility.

## Handoffs

- to Legal for applicability and obligations,
- to data protection for personal data,
- to management for risk acceptance and resources,
- to internal auditors for evidence readiness.
