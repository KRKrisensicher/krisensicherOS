# 02 — Roles, Responsibility and Management Mandate

## Purpose

This module translates responsibility into concrete roles, decision rights, deputies, escalation and review obligations.

## Reference Framework

- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 — Sections 5.1 to 5.3 and 7.1.
- BSIG § 38: <https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bsig_2025/__38.html>
- EnWG § 5e where energy-related.

## Users and Roles

- executive management,
- ISMS owner,
- information security officer/CISO,
- risk owner,
- control owner,
- evidence owner,
- management review facilitator.

## Trigger

- missing ISMS mandate,
- unclear risk acceptance,
- measures without an owner,
- management review,
- executive management training,
- organizational change.

## Process

1. Formulate the management mandate: objective, scope, priorities, resource framework.
2. Create a role matrix: decide, prepare, execute, review, escalate.
3. Define deputies and escalation paths.
4. Assign risk acceptance and budget decisions to management.
5. Plan the training and review routine for the leadership level.
6. Track role changes in the decision log.

## Decisions

| Decision | Decision-Maker | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ISMS mandate | Executive management | Mandate note |
| Role assignment | Executive management / department management | Role matrix |
| Risk acceptance right | Executive management | Decision log |
| Resource approval | Executive management | Resolution / budget note |
| Escalation path | ISMS owner + management | Escalation map |

## Evidence

- role matrix,
- management mandate,
- training evidence,
- decision log,
- resource and priority resolutions,
- review calendar.

## Review

Check whether roles can actually make decisions. A role without time, mandate or escalation path is not a robust responsibility.

## BSIG/NIS2 Reference

For affected entities, leadership responsibility, training and oversight are particularly relevant. This module prepares the required operating logic without providing a binding interpretation of obligations.

## Boundaries

Agents can propose roles, but cannot assume responsibility or make management decisions.

## Handoffs

- to management review,
- to HR/training for training evidence,
- to legal for executive management obligations,
- to risk owner for risk acceptance.
