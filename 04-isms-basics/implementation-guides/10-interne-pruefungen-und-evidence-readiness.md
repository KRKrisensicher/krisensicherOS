# 10 — Internal Reviews and Evidence Readiness

## Purpose

This module prepares internal reviews so that they do not only look for errors, but increase decision readiness and improvement capability.

## Reference Framework

- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 — sections 9.1, 9.2, 9.3, and 10.
- BSIG § 30 and § 38 in case of possible applicability.

## Users and Roles

- internal review or audit role,
- ISMS owner,
- evidence owner,
- control owner,
- management review facilitator,
- business-unit and service owners.

## Triggers

- planned internal review,
- management review preparation,
- incomplete evidence pack,
- finding or incident,
- new scope,
- external review or customer requirement.

## Process

1. Define the review objective: routine, risk, measure, scope, or evidence pack.
2. Formulate review questions: What would need to be visible if the routine works?
3. Request evidence: use existing evidence first, do not create unnecessary additional work.
4. Conduct sampling or review.
5. Sort findings by decision requirement.
6. Hand over findings with owner, cause, measure, deadline, and effectiveness review.
7. Escalate points relevant to management.

## Decisions

| Decision | Options | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evidence sufficient? | yes / partial / no | Evidence review |
| Finding relevant? | critical / material / observable | Review note |
| Measure needed? | yes / no / management decision | Finding report |
| Effectiveness review | date / method / owner | Corrective action plan |

## Evidence

- review plan,
- review questions,
- evidence request list,
- review notes,
- finding report,
- corrective action plan,
- effectiveness review,
- management handoff.

## Review

Internal reviews are useful when they trigger clear decisions: measure, acceptance, escalation, or improvement of the routine.

## BSIG/NIS2 Reference

For potentially affected entities, internal reviews can help prepare risk management and executive reviews. This does not create an external evidence or compliance statement.

## Boundaries

No certification assurance, no external audit confirmation, no legal interpretation, no reproduction of licensed content.

## Handoffs

- to corrective action planner,
- to evidence pack review,
- to management review,
- to control owner,
- to legal/data protection for regulatory findings.
