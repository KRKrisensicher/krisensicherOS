# 01 — Context, Scope and Applicability

## Purpose

This module makes visible which organizational segment the ISMS is operated for and which external requirements might be relevant as a working assumption.

## Reference Framework

- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 — Sections 4.1 to 4.4.
- BSIG § 28: <https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bsig_2025/__28.html>
- BSIG Annex 1: <https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bsig_2025/anlage_1.html>
- BSIG Annex 2: <https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bsig_2025/anlage_2.html>
- EnWG § 5c to § 5e where energy is relevant.

## Users and Roles

- ISMS owner,
- executive management,
- business unit and service owners,
- Legal/legal function,
- data protection,
- NIS2 scope precheck analyst.

## Trigger

- ISMS restart,
- new organizational unit, service, site, or supply chain,
- NIS2/BSIG preliminary check,
- material change in the business model,
- audit or customer requirement.

## Workflow

1. Capture organizational profile: offerings, sites, services, customers, critical processes.
2. Define starting scope: what is included, what is deliberately not included?
3. Capture interested parties as the decision environment: management, customers, supervisory authorities, suppliers, employees.
4. Check preliminary applicability: sector, type of entity, size, special regime.
5. Hand off open legal questions to Legal.
6. Document the scope decision in the decision log.

## Decisions

| Decision | Options | Human Gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starting scope | narrow / medium / expanded | Executive management |
| BSIG working assumption | possible match / unclear / currently no match | Legal |
| Energy special check | needed / not needed / open | Legal + business owner |
| Scope expansion | now / later / not | Management |

## Evidence

- Scope canvas,
- organizational and service overview,
- applicability questionnaire,
- legal handoff,
- decision log,
- list of open assumptions.

## Review

Review at least for new services, M&A, outsourcing, site changes, new thresholds, sector change, incident, or management decision.

## BSIG/NIS2 Reference

This module connects ISMS scope with NIS2/BSIG preliminary logic. The result remains a working assumption and must be legally reviewed.

## Boundaries

No determination of whether an organization is applicable or not applicable. No legal interpretation.

## Handoffs

- to `nis2-scope-precheck-analyst`,
- to Legal/lawyer,
- to management review for scope or resource questions.
