# 03 — Risk steering and action planning

## Purpose

This module makes risks decision-ready: risks are described, assessed, treated, tracked, and linked to actions and evidence.

## Reference framework

- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 — Sections 6.1, 6.2, and 8.1 to 8.3.
- BSIG § 30: <https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bsig_2025/__30.html>

## Users and roles

- ISMS Owner,
- Risk Owner,
- Asset or Service Owner,
- Control Owner,
- Management,
- Risk-and-Obligation-Prioritizer.

## Trigger

- new risk,
- changed scope,
- incident or finding,
- new legal or contractual requirement,
- due risk review,
- management decision on resources or residual risk.

## Process

1. Describe the risk scenario: cause, impact, affected service, owner.
2. Record existing safeguards and gaps.
3. Assess the risk and document the rationale.
4. Select treatment: reduce, avoid, transfer, accept, or defer with a deadline.
5. Create actions as a backlog with owner, deadline, evidence, and review point.
6. Record residual risk and open management decision.

## Decisions

| Decision | Options | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prioritize risk? | high / medium / low / monitor | Risk register |
| Treatment | reduce / avoid / transfer / accept / defer | Action plan |
| Resource needed? | yes / no / open | Decision Brief |
| Accept residual risk? | yes / no / time-limited only | Management decision |

## Evidence

- Risk register,
- assessment note,
- action backlog,
- Risk-Control Map,
- Decision Log,
- review minutes.

## Review

Risks must be reviewed for new services, supplier changes, incidents, findings, expired action deadlines, and at least in the defined review cadence.

## BSIG/NIS2 reference

For affected entities, § 30 BSIG can serve as a public reference anchor for risk management measures. This module helps translate measures into roles, routines, and evidence.

## Limits

No binding risk assessment, no risk acceptance by agents, no statement on the fulfillment of legal obligations.

## Handoffs

- to Control Owner for implementation,
- to Management for residual risk or resources,
- to Evidence Owner for evidence,
- to Legal for regulatory interpretation questions.
