# Fictional example: internal information security officer/CISO function

## Fictionality

This example is entirely fictional. It contains no real people, teams, organizations, incidents, metrics, or confidential information.

## Initial situation

A fictional internal information security officer/CISO function has many parallel requirements but little time. The goal is to use agents for preparation, consolidation, and quality assurance without delegating responsibility.

## Goal

Reduce the workload of CISO/information security officer work through clear agent assignments, evidence review, risk prioritization, and management review preparation.

## Example workflow

1. Collect a weekly input list from risks, findings, measures, and evidence.
2. Agents prioritize by impact, urgency, evidence needs, and management relevance.
3. The evidence reviewer checks which evidence is decision-ready.
4. The management review facilitator creates the agenda and decision questions.
5. The quality reviewer removes overclaims, bureaucracy, and unclear handoffs.
6. The information security officer/CISO decides what goes into the management review.

## Example output

### Weekly focus

| Topic | Decision needed | Evidence status | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| open risk from supplier dependency | Priority and owner | partial | Prepare management review |
| Control without current evidence | Review cadence | incomplete | Update evidence pack |
| Policy draft without operating logic | Defer approval | not decision-ready | Add owner/trigger/review |

### Workload reduction logic

- Agents prepare lists, mappings, and briefings.
- Information security officer/CISO reviews top decisions instead of raw material.
- Management decides resources, risk acceptance, and priorities.
- Evidence packs demonstrate reviewability, not perfection.

## Boundaries

Agents do not replace CISO/information security officer responsibility. They do not make risk acceptance decisions, do not provide legal or data protection assessments, and do not create certification or compliance assurance.
