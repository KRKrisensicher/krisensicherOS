# 06 — Operational ISMS Operation

## Purpose

This module connects policies, risks, measures, tickets, reviews, and management decisions into an ongoing operating rhythm.

## Reference Framework

- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 — Sections 8.1 to 8.3 as well as references to 6, 7, 9, and 10.
- BSIG § 30 and § 38 in case of possible applicability.

## Users and Roles

- ISMS owner,
- risk owner,
- control owner,
- evidence owner,
- measure owner,
- management review facilitator.

## Triggers

- due risk or measure reviews,
- new deviation,
- new service,
- new supplier dependency,
- open management decision,
- evidence pack outdated.

## Process

1. Define the operating calendar: risk, measures, evidence, management review.
2. Maintain the backlog: measures, owners, deadlines, blockers, evidence output.
3. Record deviations and exceptions.
4. Hand decision-ready topics over to management review.
5. Update the evidence pack.
6. Convert improvements into follow-up tasks.

## Decisions

| Decision | Options | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Review cadence | monthly / quarterly / event-driven | Operating calendar |
| Escalate blockers? | yes / no / wait with deadline | Measure review |
| Allow exception? | yes, time-limited / no / management decision | Exception log |
| Evidence pack sufficient? | yes / incomplete / not reviewable | Evidence review |

## Evidence

- ISMS operating calendar,
- measure backlog,
- review minutes,
- deviation and exception logs,
- evidence pack index,
- decision log.

## Review

Operational operation is effective when open items become visible and decisions do not remain vague.

## BSIG/NIS2 Reference

For potentially affected entities, the operating calendar supports ongoing evidence, risk, and leadership responsibility. Legal classification remains a human gate.

## Boundaries

Not a substitute for specialist operations, not a complete security concept, no external confirmation.

## Handoffs

- to management review,
- to IT/OT and service operations,
- to incident readiness,
- to internal reviews.
