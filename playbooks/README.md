# Playbooks

Repeatable procedures for governance, NIS2, ISMS, incident, crisis, and review situations.

Playbooks connect situation, objective, roles, sequence, decisions, escalation, outputs, follow-up, and lessons learned.

## Basic set v1.0

- `nis2-readiness-startworkshop.md`
- `monthly-security-governance-review.md`
- `minimum-viable-isms-setup.md`
- `isms-risikoworkshop.md`
- `bcms-readiness-starter.md`
- `incident-escalation-first-assessment.md`
- `nis2-incident-melde-triage.md`
- `nis2-management-schulung-und-review.md`
- `tabletop-ransomware.md`
- `evidence-pack-prep.md`
- `management-review-prep.md`

## Boundaries

- does not provide legal advice
- does not provide data protection advice
- does not provide certification or compliance assurance
- no live crisis leadership by agents
- no management decision by agents
- no real customer, personal, or confidential organizational data in public examples
