# Start BCMS readiness

## Situation / Trigger

Use when an organization wants to capture or update critical processes, dependencies, and recovery assumptions for the first time.

## Goal

The playbook prepares a BCMS start: name critical processes, capture dependencies, review recovery assumptions, and mark open management decisions. Agents structure the work but do not assume responsibility.

## Roles

- BCMS owner: defines scope, process selection, and review date.
- Process owner: describe critical services, minimum operations, and dependencies.
- IT/facility/supplier responsible parties: provide technical and organizational recovery assumptions.
- Reviewer: checks whether assumptions, evidence gaps, and open decisions are visible.
- Management: decides priorities, resources, risk acceptance, and escalations.

## Input data

- list of critical processes or services
- known dependencies on IT, personnel, sites, service providers, and data
- existing emergency, crisis, or recovery documents
- assumptions on RTO/RPO or minimum operations
- open resource and acceptance questions

## Process

1. Select critical processes and record what is out of scope.
2. For each process, capture minimum service, outage impact, and recovery target.
3. Document dependencies and assumptions with owner and source.
4. Mark gaps that need a decision or additional evidence.
5. Define management questions, measures, and the next review date.

## Escalation

Escalate if:

- legal or data protection assessment is needed,
- risk acceptance or a budget decision is needed,
- critical evidence is missing,
- roles or owners remain unclear,
- external communication is being prepared,
- live incident or crisis decisions are affected.

## Outputs

- review or workshop note
- completed templates
- measures backlog
- Evidence Needs List
- Decision Log
- Lessons Learned
- handoff to appropriate agents, skills, or human roles

## Follow-up

- Define measures with owner and deadline.
- Update Decision Log.
- Add Evidence Pack or register entries.
- Set next review cadence.
- Transfer Lessons Learned to the improvement list.

## Boundaries

- does not provide legal advice
- does not provide data protection advice
- does not provide certification or compliance assurance
- no management decision by agents
- no live crisis management
- no real customer, personal, or confidential organizational data in public examples
