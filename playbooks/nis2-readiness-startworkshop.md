# NIS2 readiness start workshop

## Situation / Trigger

Use when an organization wants to start NIS2 readiness as operational gap work.

## Goal

The workshop separates scope questions, source references, existing routines, evidence gaps, and management decisions. It does not provide a legal assessment and does not provide compliance assurance.

## Roles

- NIS2 owner or information security officer/CISO: defines scope, objective, and escalation questions.
- Regulatory Source Mapper: collects public source references without licensed full texts.
- Process and control owner: describe existing routines and evidence.
- Evidence Reviewer: checks whether gaps are evidence-based and decision-relevant.
- Management: decides priorities, resources, and risk acceptance.

## Input data

- Organization and service scope
- public source references and internal register entries
- existing policies, routines, controls, and evidence
- open findings, risks, or customer requirements
- stop points for legal, data protection, and management

## Process

1. Separate scope and applicability question; mark legal interpretation as a human gate.
2. Capture source references and internal register entries without copying standard texts.
3. Review existing routines and evidence against work fields.
4. Formulate gaps as role, routine, evidence, or decision problems.
5. Define management questions and next evidence requirements.

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- legal or data protection assessment is needed,
- risk acceptance or budget decision is needed,
- critical evidence is missing,
- roles or owners remain unclear,
- external communication is being prepared,
- live incident or crisis decisions are affected.

## Outputs

- Review or workshop note
- completed templates
- action backlog
- evidence needs list
- decision log
- lessons learned
- handoff to suitable agents, skills, or human roles

## Follow-up

- Define actions with owner and deadline.
- Update decision log.
- Add evidence pack or register entries.
- Set next review cadence.
- Transfer lessons learned to improvement list.

## Boundaries

- does not provide legal advice
- does not provide data protection advice
- does not provide certification or compliance assurance
- no management decision by agents
- no live crisis leadership
- no real customer, personal, or confidential organizational data in public examples
