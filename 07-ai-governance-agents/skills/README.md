# Skills

Skills are repeatable workflows for krisensicherOS. They translate governance tasks into concrete steps with inputs, outputs, verification, handoffs, and human approval points.

They are not knowledge repositories, not mere prompts, and not a substitute for accountable roles.

## Standard

All skills follow `07-ai-governance-agents/standards/skill-standard-v1.0.md` (`../standards/skill-standard-v1.0.md`).

Path rule:

```text
07-ai-governance-agents/skills/<skill-name>/SKILL.md
```

## Principles

A good skill:

- solves a clearly scoped governance task,
- names the required input data,
- guides through a repeatable workflow,
- produces concrete output artifacts,
- includes verification and quality gates,
- identifies human-in-the-loop points,
- avoids legal, data protection, certification, and compliance claims,
- uses only fictional examples and public-safe content.

## Start List v1.0

### `governance-operating-model`

Translates requirements into roles, routines, decision paths, escalations, and evidence flows.

### `nis2-gap-assessment`

Structures NIS2 readiness work with assessment questions, gaps, evidence needs, measures, and management decisions — without legal or compliance promises.

### `minimum-viable-isms`

Designs a Minimum Viable ISMS as a scope, risk, control, evidence, and review routine.

### `evidence-pack-review`

Reviews evidence packs for completeness, traceability, assumptions, gaps, quality, and decision readiness.

### `management-review-prep`

Prepares management reviews with agenda, decision questions, risks, options, measures, and follow-up.

### `incident-escalation-playbook`

Creates incident escalation logic with triggers, roles, communication points, decisions, and post-incident review.

### `tabletop-exercise-design`

Develops tabletop exercises with scenario, learning objectives, roles, injects, observation points, and after-action review.

### `risk-prioritization`

Prioritizes risks, gaps, and measures by impact, urgency, evidence needs, effort, and management relevance.

### `isms-risk-analysis`

Guides interactively through ISMS risk analyses: describe risk as vulnerability + asset + threat, capture the current measures situation, assess gross risk, prepare strategy, derive measures and SoA mapping, assess net/residual risk, and generate reporting.

### `bcms-readiness-starter`

Starts BCMS readiness with critical processes, dependencies, impact assumptions, restart routines, evidence, and management decisions.

### `board-briefing-generator`

Generates decision-oriented board/management briefs from reviewed risks, evidence, options, and open decisions.

### `audit-questionnaire-builder`

Creates internal audit questionnaires from requirements, source/register entries, standard/control mappings, guiding questions, and evidence objectives.

### `audit-test-procedure-mapper`

Maps audit questions to suitable test methods, sampling logic, evidence types, assessment notes, and escalation points.

### `audit-finding-writer`

Writes findings, deviations, observations, and improvement opportunities from reviewed evidence.

### `document-gap-analysis`

Compares documents with requirements, new specifications, and register entries, and classifies the need for change.

### `interview-to-document-drafter`

Creates governance document drafts from interviews, bullet points, and raw material according to structure, target group, and review points.

### `evidence-request-list-builder`

Creates evidence request lists from requirements, audit questions, test programs, and evidence pack objectives with owners, deadlines, quality notes, and human gates.

### `control-test-designer`

Designs testable control tests with test objective, method, sampling logic, evidence, assessment notes, and escalation points.

### `corrective-action-planner`

Translates findings, gaps, and deviations into concrete corrective actions with cause, owner, deadline, evidence, decision point, and review logic.

### `remediation-effectiveness-review`

Reviews completed measures for implementation evidence, effectiveness indicators, residual risk, need for reopening, and management handoff.

Note: Policy/control drafts and AI-assisted compliance workflows are covered in v1.0 through existing skills, workflows, and governance documents, not maintained as separate skill folders.

## Quality Gates

All skills must be checked against `06-evidence-management-review/06-evidence-management-review/evals/quality-gates.md` (`../../06-evidence-management-review/evals/quality-gates.md`).

Minimum requirements:

- Public Safety,
- Claim Safety,
- operating logic,
- Empowerment,
- Workload,
- Portability,
- Skill Gate.

## Stop Points

Skill work stops at:

- real personal, customer, or organizational data,
- confidential contract content,
- licensed standard texts,
- legal or data protection advice,
- compliance, certification, or security guarantees,
- management decisions,
- external publication or license/disclaimer changes without approval.
